First steps

From installation to your first certified day.

  1. Install DutyPal from Google Play on your Android phone.
  2. Set up your profile. Enter your name, carrier, main office, home terminal time zone and the cycle rules that apply to you (for example 70 hours / 8 days). These details appear on your daily record.
  3. Allow location and notifications. For AUTO mode to work with the screen off, allow location (including in the background) and notifications. A foreground notification is shown while it is active.
  4. Log in to your truck to start a recording session. Choose your truck on the main screen and log in. The REC indicator shows that a session is being recorded, and your miles and records are tied to that vehicle. When you finish, tap Log out.
  5. Set your current duty status with OFF, SB, D or ON. The break, drive, shift and cycle clocks start from there.
  6. Drive. In AUTO mode DutyPal switches to Driving when the vehicle moves. Check the Log tab and correct anything that is wrong.
  7. Review and certify each day in Compliance when your log is right.
  8. Be ready for inspection. Open Inspection in the Compliance tab, or export a PDF and share it.
Safety first. Do not use the App while driving. Warnings and calculations are estimates and may be wrong or late. You remain responsible for complying with the regulations, and a driver who must keep records of duty status must use a registered ELD unless an exception applies.
